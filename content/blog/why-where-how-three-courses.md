You work a shift in London or Paris, Rotterdam or Lisbon, Madrid or Brussels. You deliver a good death every working day. Then you call home — and your family is dying inside a system that does not have what you have.

**You live on both sides of it.** Our free community's *Global Problems* collection is the catalogue of what the evidence shows is broken: three courses, each built on its own evidence base, each written for you.

## Three questions

- **The Conspiracy of Silence asks why.** Why diaspora families suffer at end of life. Why a terminal diagnosis vanishes somewhere between the family, the physician, and a system with no room for the conversation. Why migration does not dissolve it. *#BreakTheSilence*
- **The Worst Places to Die asks where.** Where that suffering concentrates, and why in twelve specific countries. Ten structural factors, three feedback loops, and a number for the cost of dying. *#DeathDebtIsStructural*
- **The Six-Headed Hydra asks how.** How the same pattern replicates across six colonial language pipelines — English, French, Belgian, Dutch, Portuguese, Spanish — and how it regenerates. *#FightTheHydra*

## Three silences

Each course is built to break one silence:

1. **Silence about death.** What it makes sayable: *my family needs to plan.*
2. **Silence about money.** What it makes sayable: *we need a financial plan for care.*
3. **Silence about advocacy needs.** What it makes sayable: *we should learn how to advocate together.*

## The order we recommend

Why. Where. How.

**Why** comes first because it is personal. **Where** comes second because it is structural — and structural stays academic until you have faced that it exists. **How** comes last because you cannot see the system regenerate until you have seen where it operates.

Nothing is gated. Take any course alone; each stands on its own, and each becomes more legible held alongside the other two.

## What you leave with

Each course delivers one stage of readiness: *The Conspiracy of Silence* gives you **permission**, *The Worst Places to Die* gives you **clarity**, and *The Six-Headed Hydra* gives you **connection**. They are the ground under the decisions you will make later — not the decisions themselves.

And the collection asks very little of you: **recognition. That is enough.** None of these courses asks you to break the silence in your own family, challenge a parent or a sibling, or lead institutional change. Action is invited, not required.
