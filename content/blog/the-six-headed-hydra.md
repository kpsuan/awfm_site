You're living inside a system that is hundreds of years old. Our free course *The Six-Headed Hydra* asks **how** the end-of-life suffering of diaspora families keeps happening — and why it keeps coming back.

## Six pipelines

The same pattern replicates across six colonial language pipelines — **English, French, Belgian, Dutch, Portuguese, and Spanish** — each connecting source countries to destination countries. Healthcare workers flow one way; the gap in end-of-life care stays behind.

The course shows where your own family's history sits inside a specific pipeline.

## Why it regenerates

Like the hydra of myth, cut off one head and another grows back. Solving one pipeline does not solve the problem, because all six share a root: **natal alienation** — the severing of people from the ties and inheritance that would protect them.

The course also names the **Social Death Convergence**: historical and clinical social death arriving at the same bedside.

## The silence it breaks

Each of our Global Problems courses is built to break one silence. This one breaks the **silence about advocacy needs** — and makes one sentence sayable in your family: *we should learn how to advocate together.*
